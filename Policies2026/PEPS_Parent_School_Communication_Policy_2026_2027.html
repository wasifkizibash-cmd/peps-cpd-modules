To organise communication and cooperation between the school and families, built on openness, respect, and partnership.

1. Purpose & Objectives

Providence English Private School (PEPS) recognises that a strong partnership between school and home is central to every student's success and wellbeing. This policy exists to organise communication and cooperation between the school and families, so that information reaches parents and guardians clearly, consistently, and through the right channel at the right time.

The objectives of this policy are to:

  • Ensure parents and guardians are kept well informed about their child's learning, progress, behaviour, and wellbeing;
  • Provide clarity on which channel to use for which type of message, for both the school and for families;
  • Set clear expectations for timeliness, tone, and professionalism in all school-home communication;
  • Protect the confidentiality and accuracy of information shared about students and families;
  • Strengthen the partnership between school and home in support of every student's education.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all communication between PEPS staff — teachers, Section Supervisors, the Student Counsellor, Social Worker, administrative staff, middle and senior leaders, and the Principal — and parents or guardians, across every channel used by the school. It applies equally to routine academic communication, pastoral and safeguarding-related communication, and school-wide announcements.

This policy does not cover communication between staff and students directly (see the Digital Citizenship Policy and Safeguarding & Child Protection Policy), nor internal staff-to-staff communication that does not involve parents.

3. Legal & Regulatory Framework

This policy is written in accordance with the guidance and expectations of the Sharjah Private Education Authority (SPEA) and the UAE Ministry of Education (MOE) regarding parental engagement and school transparency, and operates alongside PEPS's Data Protection & Privacy Policy, which governs how personal data shared through these channels is collected, used, and protected.

4. Guiding Principles

  • Openness: parents are kept informed about matters affecting their child in a timely and transparent way.
  • Two-way partnership: communication is not one-directional; parents are encouraged and enabled to raise questions, share information, and engage with the school.
  • Right channel, right message: routine, urgent, formal, and celebratory communication each has an appropriate channel, set out in this policy.
  • Respect and professionalism: all communication, from the school and from families, is expected to be courteous and respectful, consistent with the school's Code of Conduct.
  • Confidentiality: information about a student or family is shared only with those who need it, and never posted or discussed in a way that identifies a student without appropriate consent.
  • Consistency: communication follows the same standards and channels across all year groups and sections of the school.

5. Roles & Responsibilities

Class/Subject Teachers
Responsible for day-to-day academic communication with parents through Nexsquare.io, including the weekly diary (homework and class work for the coming week), general classroom updates, and responding to routine parent queries about their subject or class.

Section Supervisors
Responsible for pastoral, attendance, and behaviour-related communication within their section, and for coordinating communication where a matter involves more than one teacher.

Student Counsellor & Social Worker
Responsible for sensitive, wellbeing, and safeguarding-related communication with families, which may take place by phone or in a private face-to-face meeting rather than through open digital channels.

Senior & Middle Leaders and Admin Staff
Responsible for whole-school announcements, formal correspondence (e.g. policy changes, admissions, fees), and direct email communication with parents on matters requiring a formal record.

Parents & Guardians
Expected to keep their contact details (email, phone number) up to date with the school office, to check Nexsquare.io and email regularly, and to engage respectfully and constructively with staff.

6. Overview of Communication Channels

PEPS uses six main channels of communication with families, each suited to a different type of message. A summary table is provided at Appendix A. The channels are:

  • Nexsquare.io — the school's primary parent communication and information platform;
  • Direct email to parents' registered email addresses;
  • SMS, used only where a matter requires urgent attention;
  • The school website (www.peps.ae) for news and sharing of school activities;
  • Official school social media accounts (X, Instagram, Facebook, and others);
  • Walk-in and face-to-face meetings, by appointment.

7. Nexsquare.io Platform

Nexsquare.io is PEPS's core communication platform and is the primary channel through which staff and parents interact on a day-to-day basis. Teachers, Section Supervisors, the Student Counsellor, Social Worker, and other staff communicate with parents — and, where useful, with one another — through the platform.

The following are communicated to parents via Nexsquare.io:

  • Internal assessment results and external/board examination results;
  • The examination timetable;
  • The weekly diary, setting out homework and class work for the following week;
  • General class and subject updates, reminders, and routine notices;
  • Attendance notifications and behaviour records, where applicable.

Every family is issued with login access to Nexsquare.io on admission, and parents are expected to check the platform regularly. Any technical difficulty accessing the platform should be reported to the school office so that access can be restored promptly.

8. Direct Email Communication

Senior leaders, middle leaders, and administrative staff communicate with parents by direct email to their registered address for matters that are formal, individual, or require a documented record — for example, admissions correspondence, fee-related matters, formal meeting invitations, and policy or procedural updates.

Parents are responsible for keeping their registered email address current with the school office; the school is not responsible for messages that go undelivered because of an outdated address on file.

9. SMS Communication

SMS messaging is reserved for matters requiring urgent, time-sensitive attention — for example, an early school closure, a safety-related announcement, or an urgent request for a parent to contact or collect their child. SMS is not used for routine academic or administrative communication, so that parents can rely on receiving an SMS as a signal that immediate attention is needed.

10. School Website & News

The school website (www.peps.ae) is used to share news, achievements, and school-wide activities with the wider school community, including prospective families. It is a public-facing channel and is not used for communication about individual students.

11. Social Media

PEPS maintains official social media accounts (including X, Instagram, and Facebook) to celebrate school life and share activities and achievements with the wider community. Only content approved through the school's official channels is posted on these accounts.

  • Social media is a one-to-many, public celebratory channel and is never used to communicate individually about a student's academic performance, behaviour, or any confidential matter.
  • Photographs or videos of students are only posted in line with the consent recorded in the school's Data Protection & Privacy Policy and admissions paperwork.
  • Parents wishing to raise a concern or query should not do so via comments or direct messages on social media; these should be directed to the school through the appropriate channel in this policy (see Appendix A).

Further guidance is provided in Appendix B.

12. Walk-In & Face-to-Face Meetings

Parents are welcome to request a face-to-face meeting with a teacher, Section Supervisor, or member of the leadership team. To ensure staff availability and appropriate preparation, meetings should be scheduled in advance through the school office, Nexsquare.io, or by email, rather than as unscheduled walk-ins during lesson time.

Where a parent arrives without an appointment, staff will make reasonable efforts to assist, or to arrange a suitable time as soon as possible, in line with the School Visitors Code of Conduct governing all visits to the premises.

13. Response-Time Expectations

Staff aim to respond to routine parent communication (via Nexsquare.io or email) within two school working days. More complex queries may take longer to resolve fully, but parents will receive an acknowledgement within this timeframe confirming the matter is being looked into. Urgent, safety-related matters are responded to as a priority, without delay.

Staff are not expected to respond to non-urgent communication outside of normal school hours, at weekends, or during school holidays.

14. Emergency & Urgent Communication

In an emergency or time-critical situation (for example, an unplanned school closure, a safety incident, or a lockdown/evacuation), the school will use a combination of SMS, Nexsquare.io, and, where time allows, email to ensure parents are informed as quickly and clearly as possible. Parents are strongly encouraged to ensure their contact details are accurate at all times so that they can be reached in such situations.

15. Confidentiality & Data Protection

All communication about an individual student is treated as confidential and shared only with that student's parents or guardians (or another party with appropriate legal authority), and only through the appropriate channel set out in this policy. Contact details, academic records, and any other personal data shared through these channels are processed in accordance with the school's Data Protection & Privacy Policy.

Staff must not use personal mobile numbers, personal email accounts, or personal social media to communicate with parents about school matters; all communication takes place through official, school-provided channels.

16. Language & Accessibility

Key school-wide communications are made available in both English and Arabic wherever practicable. Where a family requires communication in an alternative format or through an alternative method because of a specific accessibility need, the school will make reasonable arrangements in coordination with the Inclusion team, in line with the school's Inclusion Policy.

17. The Parents' Role in Communication

Effective communication is a two-way responsibility. Parents and guardians are asked to:

  • Keep contact details (email address, mobile number) up to date with the school office;
  • Check Nexsquare.io and their registered email regularly, particularly during the school week;
  • Respond to school communication in a timely manner where a response or action is requested;
  • Communicate with staff respectfully and constructively, in line with the standards expected of all members of the school community;
  • Raise concerns through the appropriate channel rather than through students, informal channels, or social media.

18. Escalating a Communication Concern

Where a parent is not satisfied with the response received from a teacher or Section Supervisor, or feels a communication matter has not been resolved, the concern can be escalated to the relevant middle or senior leader, and if necessary, formally raised in line with the school's Complaints Policy.

19. Monitoring & Policy Review

This policy is monitored by the Vice Principal and reviewed periodically to ensure the channels and practices described remain effective, and to reflect any change in the platforms or systems the school uses. Feedback from parents on the effectiveness of school communication is welcomed and considered as part of this review.

Appendix B: Social Media Guidelines

For the School

  • Only official, approved PEPS accounts are used to represent the school on social media.
  • Content is celebratory and public in nature — achievements, events, and school life.
  • Student images/videos are only posted where consent has been given, in line with the Data Protection & Privacy Policy.
  • Individual student performance, behaviour, or any confidential matter is never referenced.
  • Comments and messages raising a specific concern are redirected to the appropriate channel in this policy, not answered publicly.

For Parents

  • Please use Nexsquare.io, email, or a scheduled meeting to raise a query or concern — not social media comments or direct messages.
  • Please be respectful in any public engagement with the school's official accounts.
  • Photos or videos taken at school events by parents should not be posted publicly if they identify other people's children, out of respect for other families' privacy.

This appendix should be read alongside PEPS's Digital Citizenship Policy and Data Protection & Privacy Policy.
